# Support

Last updated: 2026-10-09

For help, write to [support@crosstune.app](mailto:support@crosstune.app). The answers below cover the most common questions.

## How do I join the waitlist?

Enter your email address in the waitlist form on [crosstune.app](https://crosstune.app/#waitlist). You get an email when your account is ready.

## How do I sign in?

Go to [my.crosstune.app](https://my.crosstune.app). Sign in with an emailed code, with Google, or with Apple.

## How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in the app and delete your account. This deletes everything in it, including your tunes, lists, notes, and recordings. The deletion cannot be undone.

## How do I import my tunes?

Paste a list of tunes, or open a text file, from Settings or the empty catalog. [Read how importing works](/help/import).
